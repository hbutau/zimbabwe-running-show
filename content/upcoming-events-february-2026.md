February is shaping up to be an exciting month for runners in Zimbabwe! Here's a roundup of upcoming events you won't want to miss.

## Harare City Half Marathon - February 8, 2026

Join hundreds of runners for the annual Harare City Half Marathon. This scenic route takes you through some of the city's most beautiful neighborhoods.

- **Distance**: Half Marathon (21km), 10km, 5km
- **Start Time**: 6:00 AM
- **Location**: Harare Sports Club
- **Registration**: Open until February 5th

## Victoria Falls Trail Run - February 15, 2026

Experience the breathtaking beauty of Victoria Falls while challenging yourself on this trail run. A truly unique running experience!

- **Distance**: 15km Trail Run
- **Start Time**: 7:00 AM
- **Location**: Victoria Falls National Park
- **Registration**: Limited spots available

## Bulawayo Park Run Series

Every Saturday morning, join the friendly Bulawayo Park Run community for a free, timed 5km run.

- **When**: Every Saturday, 7:00 AM
- **Where**: Central Park, Bulawayo
- **Cost**: Free (registration required online)

## How to Register

Visit our events page or contact the individual race organizers for registration details. Remember to register early as many events have limited capacity!
