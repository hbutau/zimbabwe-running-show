## About Zimbabwe Running Show

Welcome to Zimbabwe Running Show, your premier source for all things running in Zimbabwe. We are passionate about promoting running as a healthy lifestyle and building a vibrant running community across the country.

### Our Mission

To inspire and support runners of all levels, from beginners taking their first steps to seasoned marathoners pushing their limits.

### What We Do

- Organize and promote running events throughout Zimbabwe
- Share news and updates about the local running scene
- Provide training tips and resources for runners
- Build a supportive community of runners

### Get Involved

Whether you're a casual jogger or a competitive athlete, there's a place for you in the Zimbabwe running community. Check out our News section to stay updated on upcoming events and join us on social media to connect with fellow runners.
